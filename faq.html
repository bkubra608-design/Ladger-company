Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Ledger works, how it tracks your real profits, and how it protects your business records.

1. General Questions

Ledger is a focused financial and micro-POS application built specifically for small business owners, artisans, home-based entrepreneurs, Instagram/WhatsApp sellers, and retail counters. Unlike bloated enterprise accounting software, Ledger provides direct visibility into your sales, net profit, orders, and inventory in seconds.

2. Product & Features

Net profit is calculated by subtracting your material/acquisition cost (COGS) from your actual realized sale price on every single transaction: Net Profit = Sale Price − Material Cost. Your percentage profit margin is calculated dynamically so you always know your true take-home earnings.

When customers send unstructured messages on WhatsApp or Instagram (e.g. "Send 2 scented candles to Karachi, recipient Sara 0300..."), you can paste the text directly into Ledger's AI Parser. The engine parses the text, extracts the item, quantity, buyer name, city, and price, and populates the sale form automatically.

Ledger separates your verified cash in hand from pending Cash-on-Delivery (COD) payments still held with courier riders. When parcels are delivered and the courier remits your funds, you simply mark the order completed to reconcile your cash balance.

Yes! Every recorded transaction generates a clean monochrome receipt formatted for standard thermal receipt printers or digital sharing via WhatsApp screenshots.

4. National Incubation Center (NIC)
