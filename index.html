The Challenge

Small Business Finances Are Usually Scattered Everywhere

Entrepreneurs spend hours juggling notebooks, Excel sheets, and WhatsApp chats just to figure out if they made money or lost money this week.

✕

The Typical Chaos

Orders get lost in WhatsApp chats. Paper notebooks don't calculate cumulative profit. Heavy accounting software like QuickBooks asks for chart of accounts, debit/credit ledgers, and double-entry reconciliations that waste hours.

  • Lost order details and delivery addresses
  • Unclear cost of goods sold (COGS)
  • Uncollected Cash-on-Delivery (COD) dues
  • Overwhelming and slow enterprise software
✓

The Ledger Way

Everything sits in one fast, lightweight interface: quick sale entry, automated profit calculation, customer dues tracking, low-stock warnings, and an AI parser for customer messages.

  • Record a sale in under 15 seconds
  • Instant net profit = Sale Price minus Material Cost
  • Track courier COD status & customer advance payments
  • Paste unstructured messages to extract complete orders
Smart Automation

How It Works

Four Steps to Complete Financial Clarity

Designed to fit seamlessly into the rhythm of your day without slowing down your sales.

1

Record

Log items, customer names, sale prices, material costs, and payment methods in seconds—or paste a message directly into the AI Parser.

2

Organize

Ledger automatically updates inventory levels, logs pending courier payments, and categorizes revenue by sales channel.

3

Understand

See exact net profit and profit margins. Instantly know which products generate the highest returns and which channels drive real cash.

4

Grow

Eliminate cash leakages, restock your best sellers before they sell out, and make informed business choices backed by real numbers.
