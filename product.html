Product Architecture

The Operating System for Micro Businesses

Ledger replaces fragmented spreadsheets, manual paper logbooks, and complex accounting setups with 5 cohesive modules purpose-built for how real entrepreneurs run their daily commerce.

1. Smart Dashboard 2. Sales Terminal 3. Orders & Cash Flow 4. Inventory 5. AI Parser
Module 01

The Executive Dashboard

Traditional business tools hide your profit behind confusing journal entries and double-entry general ledgers. Ledger gives you the three metrics that matter most every day:

Total Revenue: All gross sales across Instagram, WhatsApp, website, and physical popups.
Material Costs (COGS): The direct cost to create or acquire goods sold.
Net Profit & Margin %: Your actual take-home earnings with one-click period filtering (Today, Month, Year).

Module 02

Fast Sales Entry with Automatic Profit

Record any transaction in under 15 seconds. Every sale entry tracks:

  • Customer Details: Name, phone number, and delivery city.
  • Item & Stock Synchronization: Automatically pulls items and deducts quantities.
  • Advance & Due Breakdown: Handles partial deposits, bank transfers, and cash balances.
  • Channel Attribution: Tag orders as Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, or Walk-in.
  • Instant Printable Receipts: Formatted monochrome receipts ready for thermal printing or customer screenshots.

Module 04

Stock Control Without Complicated ERPs

Selling out of an item you didn't realize was low frustrates buyers and costs revenue. Ledger maintains a fast inventory catalog with:

  • One-Tap Stock Increment / Decrement: Add new batch deliveries or write off damaged items in a click.
  • Automatic Low-Stock Warnings: Items with stock < 5 get flagged with high-visibility warning borders.
  • Cost Tracking per SKU: Keeps material costs linked to each product so your margin math remains 100% accurate.
