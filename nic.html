Building from Quetta: Why We Joined NIC Online

Ledger was born out of firsthand interactions with small business owners, apparel tailors, home-based bakers, and handicraft artisans across Quetta, Balochistan. In our local markets, merchants were overwhelmed by clunky manual notebooks (khata registers) and losing hard-earned cash because traditional desktop POS systems were cost-prohibitive and designed for western retail chains.

To accelerate our technological vision without disconnecting from our grassroots merchants, founders Shamsa Malik and Kubra Batool joined the National Incubation Center (NIC) Cohort 9 through its dedicated Online Incubation Program. Operating from Quetta while collaborating digitally with NIC mentors, we proved that geography is no barrier to delivering modern, high-precision financial software. Today, Ledger is in Early Traction, testing real-time sales capture and automated WhatsApp order parsing with forward-thinking sellers.

Milestone Progress

The Evolution: Idea → Validation → NIC → Growth

How Ledger transitioned from observing financial struggles on social commerce channels to building a dedicated micro-POS software platform.

1
Stage 01 · Conceptualization

Idea: Recognizing the Cash Leakage Problem

Witnessed friends, home-bakers, apparel boutiques, and handicraft artisans losing track of courier COD dues, failing to calculate actual material margins, and recording orders on fragmented paper pads. Realized that heavy desktop accounting tools were too cumbersome for this new wave of digital entrepreneurs.

Milestone: Problem discovery interviews with 40+ local online merchants.
2
Stage 02 · Market Research

Validation: Proving the Need for Micro POS

Confirmed that over 80% of small sellers manage orders through informal messaging apps (WhatsApp & Instagram). Validated the core premise: small business owners do not want general ledger double-entry bookkeeping—they need quick sales capture, live inventory decrement, and real-time net profit calculation.

Milestone: Wireframed the lightweight single-page Micro POS interface.
3
Stage 03 · Engineering & Design

Product Development: Crafting Ledger

Engineered the responsive client software featuring the Fraunces display typography, high-contrast dark accent metric cards, instant math engine, print-ready receipt generator, and the groundbreaking AI Message Parser for unstructured customer chat extraction.

Milestone: Deployed live functional web app at https://ledger-wfwc.vercel.app.
4
Stage 04 · Acceleration

NIC Incubation: Cohort 9 Online Program

Selected for the prestigious National Incubation Center (NIC) Cohort 9. Because our founding team is headquartered in Quetta, we enrolled in the dedicated Online Incubation Program, actively collaborating with mentors on product validation, unit economics, regulatory compliance, and regional distribution.

Incubation Cohort: NIC Cohort 9 (Online Incubation)
Location / Operating Base: Quetta, Balochistan
Current Venture Stage: Early Traction
5
Stage 05 · Early Traction & Expansion

Market Strategy Execution & Community Growth

Currently executing our grassroots go-to-market strategy across Quetta and nationwide social commerce channels. Onboarding early adopters, testing WhatsApp automated order conversion, and integrating courier reconciliation for COD sellers.
