Feature Showcase

Built Specially for Micro Businesses

Every tool in Ledger exists because real small businesses asked for it. No fluff, no enterprise bloat—just the direct instruments you need to run your operations profitably.

📊

Smart Dashboard

High-level operational overview summarizing total gross revenue, material acquisition costs, net profit, and average order value in real time.

Real-Time Revenue • COGS • Profit
💳

Sales Management

Record in-person or remote transactions with customer names, purchased items, quantities, agreed discounts, and assigned payment types.

15-sec entry with instant receipt
📈

Profit Tracking

Never confuse high top-line revenue with true financial profit. Ledger isolates true net profit on every transaction so you know your actual take-home pay.

Margin % calculated dynamically per SKU
📋

Orders Management

Organize ongoing orders by status: In Progress, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled. Search customer names and order history in milliseconds.

In Progress → Shipped
💰

Cash Flow Tracking

Distinguishes between cash already verified in hand vs. funds stuck with couriers or unpaid customer tabs. Stay solvent and plan restocking safely.

Cash in hand vs. Pending Courier COD
📦

Inventory Management

Catalog your product lines, assign unit material costs, adjust stock levels with quick buttons, and receive visual alerts when inventory runs critically low.

One-tap increment & decrement controls
👥

Customer Tracking

Keep track of repeat buyers, contact phone numbers, and destination delivery cities across the country without a bulky corporate CRM.

Quick customer search & phone records
💵

Advance Payment Tracking

Custom orders frequently require partial upfront deposits (e.g., Rs. 1,000 advance). Ledger balances advances and calculates the remaining dues automatically.

Partial payment tags with due balances
🚚

COD Tracking

Cash-on-Delivery is the lifeblood of ecommerce in emerging markets. Track which courier parcels are in transit and reconcile courier payouts accurately.

COD Pending reconciliation
🧾

Receipt Generation

Generate clean, thermal-ready, or digital text receipts containing order items, subtotal, advance, and remaining balances ready to text or print.

Print-ready stylesheet & digital sharing
📑

CSV Export

Export all recorded transactions, item costs, and customer records to standard CSV files compatible with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

Instant .csv download with zero delay
💾

Backup & Restore

One-click full JSON backup of your entire business database. Easily move your data between devices or restore a saved snapshot anytime.

Full client-side JSON export & import
🤖

AI Message Parser

Paste conversational messages from WhatsApp or Instagram. The built-in AI parser identifies buyer name, product, quantity, price, and city in seconds.

Turns messy chat messages into structured orders
💬

WhatsApp Order Processing

Designed specifically for sellers whose primary sales storefront is WhatsApp Business. Convert WhatsApp inquiries into booked sales without typing duplicate notes.

Optimized for WhatsApp chat format
📸

Instagram Order Processing

Capture orders originating from Instagram Direct Messages, story replies, and comment inquiries with channel attribution.

Channel tagging: Instagram Direct
🧭

Sales Channel Tracking

Compare revenue distribution across channels: see what percentage of sales come from Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, or your physical counter.

Visual channel progress bars & stats
⚡

Automatic Profit Calculation

Real-time mathematical evaluation of gross profit and percentage margin before finalizing a sale. Never discount a product below your baseline cost again.

Automatic Margin Guardrails
