Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

At Ledger, we recognize that your sales figures, item costs, customer names, and profit margins are the most sensitive information your business possesses. We treat your business records with strict respect and privacy.

1. Information You Manage in Ledger

When using Ledger to run your business operations, you may input:

  • Customer contact information (names, delivery phone numbers, shipping cities)
  • Transaction data (sale prices, item descriptions, material acquisition costs, channel tags)
  • Inventory catalogs and SKU unit records
  • Customer messages processed through the AI Parser

2. Complete Data Ownership

You own all data you input into Ledger. We will never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records, profit margins, or customer lists to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Export & Portability

You are never locked into the platform. Ledger provides immediate one-click CSV export and JSON snapshot downloads so you can maintain independent offsite backups of your business records at any time.

4. Security & Transmission

All interactions with Ledger are encrypted in transit via industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). We employ least-privilege security controls to safeguard application endpoints against unauthorized intrusion.

5. Contact Privacy Officer

For privacy-related questions or data requests, please contact our team via the Contact page or email [Add Privacy Contact Email].
