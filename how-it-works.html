Operational Methodology

How Ledger Fits Into Your Daily Routine

Running a business is demanding. We designed Ledger so recording transactions takes seconds, freeing you to focus on your customers and craft.

1
Step One

Record with Speed

When a customer orders—whether on WhatsApp, Instagram, phone, or in person—log the transaction in under 15 seconds.

  • Choose the product or type item name
  • Enter the negotiated sale price and your material cost
  • Note any advance payment deposited into your bank/wallet
  • Or simply paste the raw customer DM into the AI Parser to auto-fill!
Pro tip: Generate and send an instant formatted receipt directly to the buyer's WhatsApp.

Step Two

Organize Automatically

You never have to manually adjust multiple sheets or reconcile duplicate ledgers. Ledger handles the downstream ripple effects automatically:

  • Inventory quantity decrements in real time
  • The order enters your dispatch pipeline (In Progress → Shipped)
  • Customer dues or COD receivables are scheduled for follow-up
  • Channel metrics immediately recalculate
3
Step Three

Understand Your Numbers

Gain absolute clarity over your real margins. No more anxiety at month-end wondering where all the money went.

  • Compare Today's Take-Home vs. This Month's Take-Home
  • Spot which products deliver high margins vs. which drain your cash
  • Monitor your Average Order Value (AOV) to evaluate promotions
  • Differentiate actual cash in hand from courier remittances in transit

Step Four

Grow with Confidence

Armed with accurate financial visibility, you make informed decisions that accelerate your growth rather than repeating costly guesswork.

  • Eliminate uncollected COD leaks with riders and delivery partners

  • Transition from reactive survival to structured, predictable expansion
